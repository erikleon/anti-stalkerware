antistalker

Triage the abuse. Keep the evidence. Stay in control of what happens next.

A free, local-first desktop tool for people being harassed or stalked by an ex or a stalker. It scores incoming messages so you don't have to read all of it yourself, preserves what you choose to keep in a form built to survive a chain-of-custody challenge, and — only when tightly gated — helps you research a suspected harasser. Nothing leaves your device except that last part, and it says so every time.

What it does

01

Triage

Buckets incoming messages into Needs review, Reviewed, and All, so you're not reading every message from someone who's hurting you just to find the ones that matter. Previews are blurred until you choose to reveal them, full keyboard control means you never need to touch a mouse, and every flagged thread shows the actual reason — a toxicity score, or a pattern like contact after a boundary you set. Scoring runs entirely on your device — no message ever leaves it for this.

02

Evidence vault

An append-only, encrypted vault preserves messages — including ones the sender tried to retract — alongside the raw source bytes and a hash taken the moment they were captured. That hash proves the bytes haven't changed since capture. It doesn't prove the message was authentic, that a clock was right, or that your export is complete — and the app says exactly that, every time, instead of a reassuring "tamper-proof" claim it can't back up.

03

OSINT, gated

Can attempt to identify a suspected harasser from public signals — but only for someone already flagged as abusive in your own vault, and every result is labeled an unverified lead, never a confirmed identity. This is the one part of the app that makes real network calls, and it shows a visible indicator the entire time it's running. This gate is friction against casual misuse, not proof of who someone is — the app doesn't let it imply more certainty than that.

How data moves

  LOCAL MACHINE, airgapped except where marked
   │
   ├─ INGEST   scoped to threads you pick, not your whole history
   │
   ├─ SCORE    local classifier + pattern detectors, no network
   │
   ├─ TRIAGE   neutral app name/icon, no notifications, panic-hide hotkey
   │
   ├─ VAULT    encrypted, append-only, passphrase ≠ your device login
   │
   └─ OSINT ──────────────────────── NETWORK ⚠   the one loud exception, gated + visible

Current status — read this before you rely on it

BuiltIngest, vault, triage, onboardingiMessage, Android SMS export, and IMAP, each through a real connect-scan-select wizard; a real Electron app you can build and run today.
BuiltKeyboard-driven triageArrow keys or j/k to move, Enter to open, r to mark reviewed, h to hide — one at a time or batch-selected. Previews are blurred by default.
BuiltBoundaries and inactivity auto-lockYou author boundaries and tagged phrases from Settings; the vault locks itself after an idle timeout (default 15 minutes) with a manual "Lock now" too.
By designNo coerced-unlock / duress passphraseConsidered and turned down — a decoy vault or duress wipe both have real failure modes under pressure. The actual mitigation is the panic-hide hotkey, disclosed on the app's own Support screen. See DESIGN.md.
Not builtLive OSINT signal-gatheringThe gate is real; the actual public-signal collectors behind it are deliberately not built yet — this is the highest-risk part of the project and it's not being rushed.
Not builtPackaged releasesNo signed installer yet. Right now this means cloning the repo and building it yourself — see the README.
This is early, actively-developed software, not a finished product. If your safety depends on a specific feature, verify it works the way you expect before you rely on it — the project would rather you know exactly where the gaps are than assume it's more finished than it is.

Why local-first

No account, no cloud. Everything runs on your machine. There's no server this project operates that could be subpoenaed, breached, or handed your data.
Source is public. You, or anyone you trust, can read exactly what this software does — nothing here has to be taken on faith.
Free. No price, no premium tier gating a safety feature behind payment.
Disguised on your device. The app runs under a neutral, ordinary-looking name and icon — not something that stands out in a dock or over someone's shoulder.
